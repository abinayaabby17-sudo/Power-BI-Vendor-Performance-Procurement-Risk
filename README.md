# Vendor Performance & Procurement Risk Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI portfolio project analyzes vendor performance and procurement risk using purchasing, delivery, quality, and supplier data.

The dashboard helps identify suppliers with high procurement exposure, poor delivery performance, and quality-related risks.

**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Data Source:** Excel  
**Project Type:** Practice / Portfolio Project

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer questions such as:

- What is the total procurement spend?
- How many suppliers are being evaluated?
- How many deliveries were late?
- What percentage of deliveries were on time?
- What is the overall rejection rate?
- Which suppliers have high spend but poor delivery performance?
- Which suppliers show quality-related risk?
- Which suppliers have both delivery and quality problems?

- High-spend vs poor-delivery comparison
- Late-delivery and quality-risk comparison
- Interactive supplier filtering

---

## Key Management Insights

- **Ionix Systems** represents significant procurement exposure combined with poor on-time delivery performance.
- **Global Fasteners** and **Jupiter Fabrication** show elevated quality risk, with relatively high procurement value and rejection levels.
- **Delta Precision** shows combined operational risk due to frequent late deliveries and higher rejection performance.
- Overall on-time delivery performance is **40.83%**, indicating that delivery reliability is an important area for supplier monitoring.
- The overall rejection rate is **3.05%**, making supplier-level quality analysis important alongside delivery performance.
